BIMF Tools 01

Available

Pipe penetrations,
placed in seconds.

Combined penetration plans and services penetration elevations are becoming the norm in Australia. BIMF Tools inserts penetration elements for you, so your team stops spending hours preparing them by hand.

  • Wall + slabInsert penetrations through walls and slabs, including slabs in a linked Revit model.
  • DetectionCaptures pipes on a slope and ignores cast-ins and unintended drafting errors.
  • SizingSets penetration diameter and thickness automatically. Filter the pipes by diameter range.
  • FamiliesUse your own penetration family or load the provided default.

BIMF Tools 02

In development

Ceiling height checks.

Ceiling height clashes are common on large commercial projects. This tool analyses ceiling elements against the building services, detects services modelled below the ceiling and highlights them in the current Revit view.
